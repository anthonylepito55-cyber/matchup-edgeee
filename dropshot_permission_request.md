## Why this is worth sending

Dropshot licenses **Goalserve** and covers ATP/WTA tour only — which is exactly the slice that
matters, now that the level split showed the unforced-error edge is a **WTA-tour** edge
(+10.8%, n=276, positive 2024/25/26, versus −0.4% ungated at the same level).

Two reasons this beats the Goalserve route if it works:
- **Cost.** Goalserve is $150/month. This is free.
- **It is already filtered.** Dropshot shows per-point attribution for exactly the matches we
  need; we would not be paying for ITF, juniors, Davis Cup and odds we do not use.

Two reasons it might not:
- They may simply say no, or not reply.
- They are a reseller, so their licence with Goalserve may not permit onward automated access
  even for a private user. That is their call to make, not ours to assume either way.

## Before sending — the free check that settles the bigger question
Open 3-4 completed **WTA Tour** singles matches on dropshotft.com and look at whether unforced
errors are shown and filled in.
- **Shown and populated** → Goalserve carries the field at tour level, so the $150/month feed is
  low-risk even if Dropshot says no.
- **Blank, or only on Grand Slams** → do not buy Goalserve on this basis.
That is you reading a website you are entitled to read, and it answers the question for free.
